Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Is Project SparkUp a dating app?

No, Project SparkUp is not a dating app. It's a platform designed to help college students form meaningful connections for academic and social purposes, such as finding study partners, project collaborators, and friends with complementary interests.

Who can participate in Project SparkUp events?

Currently, Project SparkUp events are exclusively for VIT students with a valid institutional email address. We verify all participants through our OTP system to maintain community integrity.

How does the matching algorithm work?

Our AI matching algorithm analyzes your responses to 20 questions based on the Big Five personality traits. It creates matches based on complementary personality characteristics and ensures you meet people who will help you grow and expand your perspectives.

How long are the interactions?

Each interaction is timed for 5 minutes. This creates a structured, low-pressure environment for conversation and helps overcome initial social anxiety. After the event, you can continue conversations with matches you connected with.

What makes Project SparkUp different from social media?

Unlike social media which often creates superficial connections, Project SparkUp facilitates meaningful face-to-face interactions based on personality compatibility and shared interests. Our structured approach eliminates the awkwardness of traditional networking while ensuring quality conversations.
