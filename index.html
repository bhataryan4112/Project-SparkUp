What is Project SparkUp?

Reimagining campus connections through AI-powered matchmaking

AI-Powered Matchmaking for Real-World Connections

Project SparkUp is not a dating app. It's an innovative matchmaking platform designed specifically for college environments to facilitate meaningful face-to-face interactions between students.

Using advanced personality analysis based on the Big Five traits, we create intentional matches that foster genuine connections among students with complementary traits and interests.

Our structured 5-minute interactions help students overcome social anxiety while building valuable networking skills that last beyond college.

How It Works

The Project SparkUp Process

Four simple steps to meaningful connections

1

Register with VIT Email

Complete a simple registration with your VIT email address, verified through a secure OTP process.

2

Personality Assessment

Answer 20 questions based on the scientifically validated Big Five personality traits.

3

QR Code Matching



At the event, scan your match's QR code to verify and begin a timed 5-minute interaction.

4

Provide Feedback



After each conversation, provide quick feedback to unlock your match's contact information.
