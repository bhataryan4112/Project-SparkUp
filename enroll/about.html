Our Story

How Project SparkUp came to be


Project SparkUp began as a solution to a common problem observed on our growing campus: with nearly 4,000 new students each year, making meaningful connections had become increasingly difficult.

Our team of psychology and computer science students recognized that traditional social events and random seat assignments weren't creating the deep, purposeful connections students needed for academic and social success.

After months of research into personality psychology and AI algorithms, we developed Project SparkUp's unique approach to matchmaking that combines scientific personality assessment with structured face-to-face interactions.

What started as a small pilot with 50 students has now grown into a campus-wide initiative, helping thousands of students find study partners, project collaborators, and friends with complementary personalities and interests.

Our Mission

To foster meaningful connections in academic environments through structured, AI-facilitated interactions that overcome traditional social barriers.

Our Vision

A campus community where every student finds their place, builds valuable relationships, and experiences the full benefit of diverse peer connections.

Our Approach

How we create meaningful connections

Personality Science

Based on the Big Five personality traits framework, our matching system identifies complementary characteristics that lead to growth and mutual understanding.

AI-Driven Matching

Our algorithm pairs you with people who will help you expand your horizons while creating a comfortable space for genuine interaction.

Structured Interaction

Time-limited, guided conversations overcome social anxiety and ensure every participant has meaningful exchanges.

Continuous Learning

Feedback after each interaction helps our system improve future matches and provides participants with insights about themselves.
