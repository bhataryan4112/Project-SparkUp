About Us

What's Project SparkUp?

"Not your regular matchmaking – It's smarter, bolder, and way more fun."

Welcome to Project SparkUp, where connections aren't left to chance. We're an offline, AI-powered matchmaking event that combines cutting-edge tech with real-world interactions to create meaningful, unforgettable connections. Whether it's new friendships, potential partners, or just people who get your vibe, we make meeting new people exciting and effortless.

Here's how it works: Our AI uses personality insights to match you with five unique people. You'll meet each of them in short, lively conversations that feel less like speed dating and more like a burst of connection. Each match is tailored to your vibe, ensuring every interaction is dynamic, fun, and full of potential.

What Makes SparkUp Different?

  • Tailored Matches: We dig into your personality, so your matches actually make sense.
  • Time-Efficient: Five matches, one hour, zero wasted time.
  • Real Connections: No screens, no distractions—just face-to-face fun.
  • Always Evolving: Your feedback powers our mission to keep getting better.

With SparkUp, it's not just about finding a match—it's about creating moments that matter. Our events are designed for exploration, discovery, and a whole lot of sparks.

Why Join Us?

Because you deserve something different. No swiping, no algorithms ghosting you—just authentic interactions curated by AI but driven by you.

Are you ready to spark something amazing? Let's make it happen.
